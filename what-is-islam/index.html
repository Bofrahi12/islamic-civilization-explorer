What Is Islam?

Category: Begin here

A simple, respectful introduction to Muslim belief, worship, the Quran, and the purpose of life.

A simple introduction

If you have ever wondered what our faith really is, you are not alone. Islam is one of the world's major religions, and more than a billion of us follow it around the world. Beyond headlines and common stereotypes, many people come to us with simple questions: What do we believe? Who is Allah? What is the Quran? And what does it mean to be a Muslim?

What does Islam mean?

The word Islam means submission to God and living according to His guidance. We believe that there is only one God, known in Arabic as Allah. Allah is not a different god; Allah is simply the Arabic word for God.

What do Muslims believe?

At the heart of our faith is the belief in one God. We also believe in God's angels, His prophets and messengers, His revealed books, the Day of Judgment, and divine decree. Our religion calls us to worship God, treat others with kindness and justice, help those in need, and live a meaningful life.

Who is Muhammad?

We regard Muhammad ﷺ as the final prophet and messenger of God. We believe he received God's revelation through the Angel Gabriel over approximately twenty-three years. These revelations were later compiled into the Quran, our holy book.

What is the Quran?

The Quran is the center of our religious life. We believe it is the word of God revealed to Muhammad ﷺ in Arabic. It speaks to us about faith, morality, worship, human relationships, history, and the purpose of life.

Why do Muslims pray?

Prayer is at the heart of our daily life. We perform five daily prayers. These prayers give us moments throughout the day to remember God, ask for guidance, express gratitude, and reconnect spiritually.

A gentle place to begin

Understanding our faith does not have to begin with complicated books or difficult terminology. Sometimes, the best place to start is with simple questions. What do we believe? Why do we pray? What does the Quran say? These are the questions we will explore together.

Common questions

What is Islam in simple terms?

Islam is the religion of submitting to the one God, Allah. Muslims believe God sent prophets — including Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and finally Muhammad ﷺ — and that the Quran is His final revelation, guiding how to worship God and live with justice and mercy.

What do Muslims believe about God?

Muslims believe in one God, Allah, without partners or intermediaries. This absolute oneness, called tawhid, is the foundation of Islam: God is eternal, all-knowing, all-powerful, and uniquely worthy of worship.

Do Muslims worship Muhammad?

No. Muslims honor Muhammad ﷺ as God's final prophet and follow his example, but worship belongs to God alone. The Quran states plainly that Muhammad is only a messenger (3:144).
