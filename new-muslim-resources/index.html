YOUR FIRST STEPS

Five practical steps

Not a strict order — begin with whatever fits your life.

1

Learn the prayer, step by step

Prayer is the first practice to build. You do not need to be perfect — start with one prayer a day and grow from there.

Prayer guide → · Prayer times tool →

2

Start reading the Quran

Begin with a trusted translation of the meanings — even a few verses a day. Understanding comes gradually.

About the Quran → · Read online: quran.com

3

Take the first 30 days slowly

New Muslims often feel overwhelmed. Our guide maps out a gentle first month: what to learn first, what can wait.

First 30 days guide →

4

Visit a mosque

Search “[your city] mosque” and drop by outside prayer times first. Say “I'm new to Islam” — you will be welcomed. Shoes off at the door, quiet inside.

What happens in a mosque? →

5

Keep asking questions

Doubts and questions are normal — the companions of the Prophet ﷺ asked constantly. Never feel embarrassed to ask.
