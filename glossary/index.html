GLOSSARY

Islamic terms,
in plain language.

Every Arabic word you meet in our guides, explained simply.

Allah الله

The Arabic word for God — the one Creator worshipped in Islam.

Quran القرآن

The revealed scripture of Islam, believed to be the word of God sent to Muhammad ﷺ.

Islam الإسلام

Submission to God; the religion of Muslims.

Muslim مسلم

One who submits to God; a follower of Islam.

Shahada الشهادة

The testimony of faith: there is no god but Allah and Muhammad is His messenger.

Salah الصلاة

The five daily ritual prayers.

Zakat الزكاة

Obligatory alms — a fixed share of wealth given to the needy.

Sawm الصوم

Fasting, especially in Ramadan from dawn to sunset.

Hajj الحج

The pilgrimage to Mecca, obligatory once for those who are able.

Umrah العمرة

The lesser pilgrimage to Mecca, which can be done any time of year.

Wudu الوضوء

Ritual washing before prayer.

Masjid المسجد

Mosque — the Muslim place of worship.

Imam الإمام

The prayer leader; also a title of religious scholarship.

Adhan الأذان

The call to prayer announced five times daily.

Khutbah الخطبة

The sermon, especially before Friday prayer.

Jumu'ah الجمعة

Friday — the day of congregational prayer.

Iman الإيمان

Faith — belief in God, angels, books, messengers, the Last Day, and divine decree.

Ihsan الإحسان

Excellence in worship — to worship God as though you see Him.

Taqwa التقوى

God-consciousness; mindful piety.

Tawhid التوحيد

The oneness of God — Islam's central doctrine.

Shirk الشرك

Associating partners with God — the gravest sin in Islam.

Sunnah السنة

The Prophet's ﷺ example in word and deed.

Hadith الحديث

A reported saying or action of the Prophet ﷺ.

Sahaba الصحابة

The Prophet's ﷺ companions.

Sharia الشريعة

Islam's divine guidance and legal-ethical path.

Fiqh الفقه

Islamic jurisprudence — scholarly understanding of the law.

Fatwa الفتوى

A scholarly legal opinion on a specific question.

Halal الحلال

Permissible according to Islamic law.

Haram الحرام

Forbidden according to Islamic law.

Dawah الدعوة

Inviting others to Islam with wisdom and good conduct.

Ummah الأمة

The worldwide Muslim community.

Jannah الجنة

Paradise — the eternal reward of the righteous.

Akhirah الآخرة

The Hereafter; life after death.

Dunya الدنيا

This worldly life.

Jinn الجن

Unseen beings created from smokeless fire, with free will.

Ramadan رمضان

The ninth lunar month; the month of fasting.

Laylat al-Qadr ليلة القدر

The Night of Decree — better than a thousand months.

Eid al-Fitr عيد الفطر

The festival marking the end of Ramadan.

Eid al-Adha عيد الأضحى

The festival of sacrifice during Hajj.

Kaaba الكعبة

The sacred cube-shaped building in Mecca facing which Muslims pray.

Qibla القبلة

The direction of prayer toward Mecca.

Dhikr الذكر

Remembrance of God through repeated phrases.

Dua الدعاء

Personal supplication to God.

Inshallah إن شاء الله

"If God wills" — said when speaking of the future.

Alhamdulillah الحمد لله

"All praise belongs to God" — said in gratitude.

SubhanAllah سبحان الله

"Glory be to God" — said in awe.

Allahu Akbar الله أكبر

"God is greater" — the phrase of the call to prayer.

MashaAllah ما شاء الله

"As God willed" — said admiring something good.

Assalamu Alaikum السلام عليكم

"Peace be upon you" — the Islamic greeting.

Bismillah بسم الله

"In the name of God" — said before beginning things.

Hijab الحجاب

Modest covering; commonly the headscarf.

Surah السورة

A chapter of the Quran.

Ayah الآية

A verse of the Quran.

Tafsir التفسير

Scholarly commentary explaining the Quran.

Seerah السيرة

The biography of the Prophet ﷺ.

Sabr الصبر

Patience and perseverance.

Tawakkul التوكل

Trust and reliance on God.

Tawbah التوبة

Repentance; turning back to God.

Sadaqah الصدقة

Voluntary charity.

Ihram الإحرام

The sacred state (and garments) for pilgrimage.

Tawaf الطواف

Circling the Kaaba seven times.
