Prophets in Islam

Muhammad in Islam

The final prophet in Islam, whose call, community, and revelation shaped the beginning of the Muslim tradition.

Focus: revelation, character, community-building, historical context, and careful source distinction.

Who was he?

Muhammad is regarded by Muslims as the final prophet and messenger of God. He received revelation through the Angel Gabriel and conveyed the Quran to his community in Arabic.

What happened in his story?

A careful introduction follows the Quran first, then distinguishes hadith reports, early biography, and later historical interpretation. Muhammad’s life includes the first revelation, persecution in Mecca, migration to Medina, community-building, treaties, and the return to Mecca.

What can we learn?

The early Muslim community is best studied with both reverence and historical care. Claims should name their source, distinguish strong reports from disputed details, and avoid using biography as a vehicle for hostility toward others.
