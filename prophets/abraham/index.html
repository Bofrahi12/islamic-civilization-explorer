Prophets in Islam

Abraham in Islam

A central Quranic figure associated with sincere monotheism, questioning inherited idols, and covenant.

Focus: sincere monotheism, thoughtful questioning, courage, and worship expressed through action.

Who was he?

Abraham (Ibrahim) is one of the most prominent prophets in the Quran. He is portrayed as a devoted monotheist who sought truth and refused to treat created things as gods.

What happened in his story?

The Quran recounts Abraham’s conversation with his people about celestial bodies and idols, his breaking of the idols, and God’s protection from the fire. It also connects him with the House and with a legacy of worship and service.

What can we learn?

Faith is not presented as an unthinking inheritance. Abraham’s story values honest reflection, courage without arrogance, and devotion expressed through action.

Quran references

  • Quran 2:124–132
  • Quran 6:74–83
  • Quran 21:51–70
