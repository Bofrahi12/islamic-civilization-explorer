David in Islam

A prophet and king in the Quranic account, associated with judgment, gratitude, and the Psalms.

Focus: fair judgment, gratitude, worship, and the responsibility that comes with authority.

Who was he?

David (Dawud) is described in the Quran as a prophet and a ruler to whom God gave wisdom and judgment. The Quran also associates him with the Zabur, commonly translated as the Psalms.

What happened in his story?

The Quran mentions David’s victory over Goliath, his judgment, his worship, and God’s gifts to him. In the account of the two disputants, he is reminded to judge with truth and not follow desire.

What can we learn?

Authority increases the need for fairness. Spiritual devotion and public responsibility are not presented as opposites, and correction should be received even by the powerful.
