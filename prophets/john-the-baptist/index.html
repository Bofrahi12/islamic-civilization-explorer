John the Baptist in Islam

Yahya, a righteous son of Zechariah, praised in the Quran for purity, compassion, and devotion.

Focus: wisdom, compassion, purity, dutifulness, and gentle moral seriousness.

Who was he?

John the Baptist is known in the Quran as Yahya. He is introduced through the prayer of Zechariah and the divine announcement of a son with a distinctive name.

What happened in his story?

The Quran praises Yahya’s wisdom, compassion, purity, and dutifulness. The story is concise; it does not provide every detail later traditions may associate with him.

What can we learn?

Moral seriousness can be gentle. Purity in the Quran is connected with mercy, responsibility, and care for parents rather than performance for an audience.

Quran references

  • Quran 3:38–41
  • Quran 19:7–15
