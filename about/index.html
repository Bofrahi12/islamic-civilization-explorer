About the project

Written by Muslims, for the curious.

Discover Islam is an educational project written by Muslims who believe Islam is the truth — and who want you to meet it as we know it: through its own sources, in its own voice. We are not neutral observers describing a religion from the outside; we are believers opening the door from the inside, and we think you deserve that honesty up front.

Our method: the Quran comes first, quoted directly. Then hadith, with authenticity grades named (sahih, hasan, da'if) so you know the strength of each report. Then history, then scholarly interpretation — and where our scholars genuinely disagree, we say so instead of pretending to a consensus that does not exist. We present mainstream Sunni Islam — the understanding held by the great majority of Muslims across history — while treating other Muslim traditions with respect. Nothing here is a personal fatwa: for religious rulings, consult a qualified scholar. We welcome thoughtful corrections and source suggestions, and we correct our mistakes openly.
