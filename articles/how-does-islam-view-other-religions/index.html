Islam & Other Religions

How Does Islam View Other Religions?

10 min read · 2026-09-23 · Category: Islam & Other Religions

Explore Quranic dialogue, religious difference, the People of the Book, peaceful relations, and the diversity of Muslim interpretation.

Difference is part of the question

We view other religions through several connected ideas: Allah's revelation and prophets, human responsibility, religious difference, dialogue, justice, and the historical relationship among communities. There is no single sentence that answers every political and theological question.

No compulsion and thoughtful dialogue

Quran 2:256 says there is no compulsion in religion. Quran 16:125 instructs our Prophet ﷺ to invite with wisdom and good instruction, and to argue in the best manner.

People of the Book and shared history

Our Quran speaks about earlier prophets and communities connected with revelation, including Jews and Christians, while also making theological claims about what it considers true. We therefore often use the phrase People of the Book for particular communities, while recognising real doctrinal disagreement.

Peaceful relations and legal history

Quran 60:8 says Allah does not forbid kindness and justice toward those who do not fight us because of religion or expel us from our homes. This supports our ordinary ethical relations with peaceful neighbours.

A fair interfaith conclusion

Our religion invites serious theological conviction while also requiring justice, courtesy, and restraint in relations with others. We may disagree about how to apply these principles in politics, law, and interfaith work.
