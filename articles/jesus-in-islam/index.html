Jesus in Islam

Jesus in Islam: What Muslims Actually Believe

Category: Jesus in Islam

A respectful introduction to Jesus, Mary, prophecy, and worship in the Quranic view.

The central idea

For us, Jesus is a prophet and messenger of God, and Mary is highly honored. We believe Jesus was born miraculously and was given signs by God.

What to take with you

We do not worship Jesus. Our faith teaches worship of the One God alone, while honoring Jesus as a revered human messenger. Our views on the crucifixion and the Messiah deserve a careful explanation, distinguished from Christian theology.

Common questions

Do Muslims believe in Jesus?

Yes — belief in Jesus (Isa) is required in Islam. Muslims honor him as one of the greatest prophets, born miraculously to the Virgin Mary, who performed miracles by God's permission and will return before the end of time.

Do Muslims believe Jesus is the Son of God?

No. Muslims love and revere Jesus but do not believe he is divine or the Son of God. The Quran affirms God's absolute oneness and describes Jesus as a noble prophet and messenger, not as God incarnate.

Do Muslims believe Jesus died on the cross?

Muslims believe Jesus was not killed nor crucified, but that God raised him up (Quran 4:157–158). Classical Muslim scholars differ on the details of what the onlookers saw, but agree that Jesus was spared and exalted by God.
