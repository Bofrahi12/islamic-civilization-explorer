What Are the Signs of the Day of Judgment?

9 min read · 2026-09-27 · Category: Islamic Beliefs

Minor and major signs of the Hour in Islam — from the Quran and authentic hadith — explained for preparation, not prediction.

The Hour is certain; its time is hidden

Let us say the most important thing first, because sensationalism has poisoned this topic: no one knows when the Hour will come — not the Prophet ﷺ, not the scholars, not the viral video. 'They ask you about the Hour: when is its arrival? Say: its knowledge is only with my Lord' (7:187; see 33:63). Anyone who sets a date is contradicting the Quran.

The minor signs

The most famous teaching on this is the hadith of Jibril (Muslim 8) — graded authentic — in which the Angel Gabriel, in human form, asks the Prophet ﷺ about the Hour, and he answers with signs: that a slave woman gives birth to her mistress, and that barefoot, destitute shepherds compete in constructing tall buildings.

The major signs

Then come the major signs — extraordinary events our tradition describes in sequence: the Dajjal, the great deceiver (Muslim 2937); the descent of Jesus, son of Mary, who will defeat him (4:159; Bukhari 3448) — yes, we believe Christ returns; Gog and Magog unleashed (21:96); and the sun rising from the west (6:158), after which repentance is no longer accepted.

The Mahdi — with scholarly care

Many Muslims await the Mahdi, a guided leader from the Prophet's ﷺ household who will fill the earth with justice. Reports about him appear in collections like Abu Dawud (4282) — though scholars differ on the grading of individual narrations, and careful students check each one.

The point of knowing: preparation, not prediction

So why learn the signs at all? The Quran answers: 'Turn to your Lord in repentance and submit to Him before the punishment comes upon you' (39:53–54). Every sign is an invitation with a deadline unknown — pray, mend relationships, give charity, seek forgiveness. The signs are not there to frighten you into paralysis but to wake you into action.

References

  • Qur'an 7:187 — knowledge of the Hour is with my Lord
  • Sahih Muslim 8 — the hadith of Jibril
  • Sahih al-Bukhari 3448 — the descent of Jesus
