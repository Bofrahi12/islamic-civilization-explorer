What Is the Quran?

Category: Quran

Meet Islam's central religious text and the questions it places before humanity.

The central idea

The Quran is the center of our religious life. We believe it is the word of God revealed to Muhammad ﷺ in Arabic.

What to take with you

It speaks to us about faith, morality, worship, human relationships, history, and the purpose of life. As you read it, it helps to distinguish the Quran itself from translations and later interpretations.

Common questions

What is the Quran?

The Quran is the book Muslims believe is the literal word of God, revealed to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over 23 years. It is recited in Arabic worldwide and covers belief in one God, worship, morality, law, and the stories of earlier prophets.

Has the Quran been changed over time?

Muslims believe the Quran has been preserved exactly as revealed. It was memorized by thousands during the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime, written down under his supervision, and compiled into a single text within about twenty years of his death; early manuscripts match the text recited today.

How should a beginner start reading the Quran?

Start with a reliable translation, beginning with the short opening chapter Al-Fatiha and the short surahs at the end of the book. Read slowly and reflectively — and remember that translations convey meaning while the Arabic recitation is the act of worship itself.
