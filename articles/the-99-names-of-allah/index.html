The 99 Names of Allah: Meaning and Beauty

8 min read · 2026-09-27 · Category: Islamic Beliefs

The 99 Names of Allah: what they mean — the Merciful, the Just, the Loving — how Muslims use them, and what they reveal about God.

'To Allah belong the most beautiful names'

Our Quran commands: 'To God belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them' (7:180). And our Prophet ﷺ said God has ninety-nine names — 'one hundred minus one; whoever learns them by heart will enter Paradise' (Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3507).

What the names mean

The names are a portrait of God painted in words: Al-Rahman (the Most Merciful), Al-Adl (the Utterly Just), Al-Wadud (the Most Loving), Al-Ghafoor (the All-Forgiving), Al-Hakeem (the All-Wise), Al-Qareeb (the Near).

How Muslims use them

We call upon God by His names in dua — 'O Merciful, have mercy on me' — exactly as 7:180 instructs. Many of us count the names on prayer beads (dhikr), letting each name settle into the heart.

What the names rule out

The names describe perfection, not human likeness: 'There is nothing like Him' (42:11). When we say God is 'the Loving' or 'the Just,' we mean these attributes in their perfect, unlimited form — utterly unlike our partial, fragile versions.

An invitation

You don't need to memorize ninety-nine names today. Learn five this week — the Merciful, the Just, the Loving, the Forgiving, the Near — and say them slowly.

References
