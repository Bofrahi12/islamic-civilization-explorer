What Is Ramadan?

9 min read · 2026-09-18 · Category: Muslim Life

A detailed, source-aware guide to Ramadan, the Islamic lunar month of fasting, worship, reflection, and community.

The central idea

Ramadan is the ninth month of our lunar calendar. We observe it around the world as a month of fasting, prayer, Quran recitation, generosity, and renewed attention to worship.

A closer look · 2

Because our calendar follows the moon, Ramadan moves through the seasons over time. Our daily fast begins before dawn and ends at sunset, followed by an evening meal often shared with family, friends, neighbors, or the wider community.

What to take with you

The month concludes with Eid al-Fitr, our festival of gratitude and communal prayer. Ramadan is experienced differently across cultures, but its central focus remains worship and moral renewal.
