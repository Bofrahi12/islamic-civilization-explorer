Who Was Muhammad?

Category: Prophets

Learn why Muslims regard Muhammad as the final prophet and messenger of God.

The central idea

We regard Muhammad ﷺ as the final prophet and messenger of God. We believe he received revelation through the Angel Gabriel.

What to take with you

A responsible biography distinguishes the Quran, hadith reports, early biography, and later historical interpretation. It also places our Prophet ﷺ within the religious and social world of seventh-century Arabia.

Common questions

Who is Muhammad?

Muhammad ﷺ is the man Muslims believe is God's final prophet, who received the Quran over 23 years in 7th-century Arabia. Known even before his mission as Al-Amin (the Trustworthy), he is honored as a mercy to the worlds and the pattern for Muslim life.

Did Muhammad write the Quran himself?

Muslims believe he did not — and could not have. The Quran itself notes he had not read a book or written with his hand before revelation (29:48), and it challenges doubters to produce a single chapter like it (2:23), a challenge Muslims hold unanswered for fourteen centuries.

What was Muhammad's character like?

Muslims describe him through the Quran's words: 'a mercy to the worlds' (21:107) with 'tremendous character' (68:4). He forgave his persecutors at the conquest of Makkah, was known for honesty, generosity, and tenderness with the poor, children, and animals.
