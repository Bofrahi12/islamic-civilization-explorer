Islam & Other Religions

What Do Muslims Think of the Bible?

8 min read · 2026-09-27 · Category: Islam & Other Religions

How we honor the original revelation, what the Quran says about earlier scriptures, and where we agree and differ with the Bible today.

Respect for the original revelation

We begin with respect. We believe God revealed the Torah to Moses and the Gospel to Jesus — real revelations, guiding their peoples. The Quran confirms this: it describes itself as confirming 'what was before it' and mentions the Torah and Gospel as guidance and light (5:46; 3:3–4). For us, these are not foreign books; they belong to the same divine conversation.

The Quran as guardian (muhaymin)

The Quran describes its own relationship to earlier scriptures with a striking word: muhaymin — a guardian, watcher, and criterion over them (5:48). We understand this to mean the Quran confirms what is true in earlier revelations and corrects what has been altered. It is the final edition, the reference point.

What 'corruption' (tahrif) means — and where scholars differ

The Quran does accuse some among the People of the Book of distorting revelation: it speaks of those who 'write the book with their hands and then say: This is from God' (2:79), and of others who 'twist their tongues with the Book' (3:78). The Arabic term is tahrif — distortion or alteration.

How Muslims read the Bible in practice

In practice, many of us read the Bible with genuine interest — especially the parts that echo Quranic teaching. When Jesus is asked for the greatest commandment and answers, 'Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God, the Lord is one' (Mark 12:29), we hear our own Shahada reflected back. These moments of agreement delight us.

A reader's path

If you are a Christian curious about our view, we suggest a simple exercise: read the Quran's accounts of Jesus and Mary alongside the Gospels, and notice both the resonances and the divergences. Ask why each tradition emphasizes what it does. Curiosity, not defensiveness, is the right posture — for us as well as for you.
