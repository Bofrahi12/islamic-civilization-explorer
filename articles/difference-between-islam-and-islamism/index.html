What Is the Difference Between Islam and Islamism?

10 min read · 2026-09-23 · Category: Common Questions

Clarify the difference between a global religion and modern political ideologies that use Islamic language, without stereotyping Muslims.

The short distinction

Islam is our global religion, with beliefs, worship, scripture, ethics, law, history, and many diverse communities. Islamism is a modern political term generally used for movements or ideologies that seek to organise political and social life through particular interpretations of Islam.

Why the definition is debated

Scholars do not use Islamism in exactly the same way. Some apply it to projects that seek an Islamic state or political order; others use it more broadly for modern movements that make Islam the basis of political mobilisation.

Religion and politics are not identical categories

Our sources address worship, ethics, social responsibility, and justice — but modern parties and states interpret those sources through institutions, constitutions, economic interests, colonial histories, and political struggles.

Islamism is not one political programme

Some Islamist movements participate in elections and civil society; others seek revolutionary change; some use militant violence; others reject it. Their goals, histories, and methods require separate evidence.

How to speak precisely

Use Islam when discussing our religion or Muslim communities, and use Islamism when discussing a clearly defined modern ideology or movement. Name the organisation, country, period, and scholarly definition whenever possible.
