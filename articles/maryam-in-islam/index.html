Mary in Islam: Who Is Maryam?

8 min read · 2026-09-27 · Category: Jesus in Islam

Meet Maryam — the only woman named in the Quran, honored with an entire chapter, and revered by Muslims as a model of faith.

An entire chapter named Maryam

Mary — Maryam in Arabic — is the only woman mentioned by name in our Quran, and its nineteenth chapter bears her name. No other woman receives this honor in our scripture.

'Chosen above the women of the worlds'

Our Quran reports angels telling Mary: 'God has chosen you and purified you and chosen you above the women of the worlds' (3:42). This is an extraordinary status — a woman elevated as an example for all humanity.

The Quranic account of Jesus's birth

Surah Maryam describes Mary withdrawing to a distant place, the pains of childbirth driving her to a palm tree, and God's provision of water and dates (19:22–26). When her people accuse her, the infant Jesus speaks from the cradle in her defense (19:29–33).

A model for all believers

Our Quran presents Maryam as 'an example for those who believe' — men and women alike (66:12). She is praised for guarding her chastity, believing in God's words, and being among the devoutly obedient.

Mary as a bridge between faiths

Mary is one of the few figures deeply revered in both Christianity and our faith — though understood differently. Christians honor her as the Mother of God; we honor her as the greatest woman of creation and the mother of a mighty prophet.
